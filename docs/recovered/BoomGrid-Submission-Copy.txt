Project description
BoomGrid is a browser party game for 2–6 players built around one simple idea: hide three numbers and hunt everyone else's. Each hidden number is a life. On your turn, choose any surviving opponent, aim at a number, and fire. The blast resolves before the next player acts; the last player alive wins.

The strategy comes from limited powers: Double Shot attacks two numbers, Suicide Bomb trades one of your lives for an opponent's, Invincibility protects you until your next turn, and Radar scans a row or column. Every board stays visible, so players can follow the action and choose their next target. Arcade explosions, original synthesized music, scrollable highlights, and table chat with typing indicators give the match its personality.

Friends join with a room code, without an account or app install. A visual guided tour and bot practice help new players learn before joining friends on a phone or laptop.

I used ChatGPT Work to build and iterate on BoomGrid, directing the rules, arcade style, tutorial, mobile layout, and attack timing through repeated feedback. The game uses React, TypeScript, Next.js, and Tailwind CSS, with a Vercel frontend and a shared game backend. Validation includes 31 automated tests and two-player production checks for joining, turn order, and synchronized hits.

QUICK DEMO
